# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard  

## Recommended Structure and Order  

### 1. Project Title / Headline  
**Hospital Emergency Room Monthly Analysis Dashboard**  
An interactive Excel dashboard designed to track patient admissions, waiting times, satisfaction levels, and department referrals—helping hospitals improve emergency room efficiency and patient care.  

---

### 2. Short Description / Purpose  
The Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard provides real-time insights into patient flow, admission status, and service efficiency. It helps hospital administrators and healthcare professionals monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify bottlenecks, and improve overall patient experience in emergency care.  

---

### 3. Tech Stack  
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:  
• 📊 **Microsoft Excel** – Main platform for data visualization and dashboard creation.  
• 📂 **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts** – Used for aggregating and analyzing patient data.  
• 📈 **Excel Formulas & Functions** – For dynamic calculations of KPIs such as average wait time and satisfaction scores.  
• 🎨 **Conditional Formatting & Charting** – To highlight trends and performance metrics visually.  
• 📁 **File Format** – `.xlsx` (Excel workbook with dashboard).  

---

### 4. Data Source  
Source: Simulated hospital emergency room dataset.  

The dataset contains records of patient visits, including details on admission status, age groups, gender, department referrals, waiting times, and satisfaction scores.  

---

### 5. Features / Highlights  

**• Business Problem**  
Emergency rooms face challenges in balancing patient flow, reducing waiting times, and ensuring high satisfaction levels. Without proper visualization, it is difficult to track performance and identify areas needing immediate improvement.  

**• Goal of the Dashboard**  
To provide an interactive visual tool that:  
- Tracks patient flow and admissions on a monthly basis.  
- Analyzes gender distribution, age groups, and department referrals.  
- Monitors key KPIs such as average wait time and satisfaction score.  
- Improves decision-making for resource allocation and patient care efficiency.  

**• Walkthrough of Key Visuals**  
- **KPI Cards (Top Panel):** Total Patients, Average Wait Time, and Satisfaction Score.  
- **Admission Status:** Split of admitted vs. not admitted patients with % share.  
- **Patient Attend Status:** On-time vs. delayed visits analysis.  
- **Gender Wise Analysis:** Male vs. Female distribution of patients.  
- **Patients by Age Group (Bar Chart):** Shows distribution across age categories.  
- **No. of Patients by Department Referral:** Highlights referrals to departments like General Practice, Orthopedics, Cardiology, Neurology, etc.  

**• Business Impact & Insights**  
- **Operational Efficiency:** Identifies waiting time patterns for better staff allocation.  
- **Patient Demographics:** Helps tailor services based on age and gender trends.  
- **Department Demand:** Highlights departments receiving the most referrals for resource planning.  
- **Quality of Service:** High satisfaction scores confirm effective emergency handling, while bottlenecks can be quickly addressed.
